Prototype:
Drag (mouse: press and move · touch: hold ~0.3s then move)
· Item → a Pokémon in My Team = use / give it
· Pokémon → a team slot = add (or replace that slot)
· Team slot → another slot = reorder · Team slot → the middle area = remove
Swipe left / right on the grid = switch tab (trackpad: two-finger swipe)
Double-tap a Pokémon = toggle ♥ favorite
Tap a sort chip again = flip ascending / descending
Sort / filter row: swipe sideways (mouse: drag or scroll wheel)
EV: open a Pokémon → EV → tap a tile = +1, hold = keep adding, Undo
Moves: open a Pokémon → Moves → tap a current move → tap a new one
Moves page: drag a move on the right onto a slot on the left to swap
Sort order: tap ✎ Order at the end of the sort row
Prof. Oak: open a Pokémon → ⇪ → hold to send (refunds its EV units + 1)
Status bar is locked in the Bag · Home = back Home (or back to the battle)
